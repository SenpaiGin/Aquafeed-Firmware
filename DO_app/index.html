    INSTRUMENT PROCEDURE

    Calibration

    The BLE-9100 manual documents calibration through the meter’s MODE/CAL key. This web app does not send an unverified calibration command.

    Use the meter’s physical controls to perform calibration. Record the completed calibration here for your operating log.
    100% · IN AIR

    Air calibration

    1. Switch the meter to saturation (%) mode.
    2. Wait for the reading to stabilize in air.
    3. Press and hold MODE/CAL on the meter for 5 seconds.
    4. Confirm the meter flashes 100.0% three times.
    0% · OPTIONAL

    Zero oxygen calibration

    1. Prepare anaerobic solution following its supplied instructions.
    2. Switch the meter to mg/L mode and wait for a stable reading.
    3. Press and hold MODE/CAL on the meter for 5 seconds.
    4. Confirm the meter flashes 0% three times.
